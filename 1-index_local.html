Stock Sense AI

NSE scanner - aapka data, aapke browser mein analysis. Kuch bhi upload ya store nahi hota.
100% client-side No data leaves your phone Apna data dijiye

Historical CSV upload karein ya paste karein - har row: symbol,date,open,high,low,close,volume (header ho ya na ho, volume optional). NSE ki site ya broker (Groww etc.) se export ki file chal jayegi. File mein jitne stocks honge, sab scan honge.

Tip: kam se kam 60 din (best: 200+ din) ki history per stock dijiye - indicators utne hi accurate honge.

Ek stock ke daily closing prices paste karein (oldest se newest, comma/space/newline separated). RSI, MACD, SMA/EMA, 52-week position, signal aur outlook turant milenge.

Signals kaise kaam karte hain

Buy / Hold / Sell signal

Har stock par 9 transparent checks: price vs 20/50/200-day averages, 50 vs 200-day trend, MACD momentum aur uski direction, RSI zone, 52-week range position, unusual volume. Bullish check +1, bearish -1. Score 4 ya zyada = BUY, -4 ya kam = SELL, beech mein = HOLD. Har stock ke andar exact wajah list hoti hai.

Short-term aur long-term outlook

Short-term (2-6 hafte): 20-day EMA, MACD momentum aur RSI zone se. Long-term (6-12 mahine): 200-day average, uski slope aur 52-week position se. Ye rule-based technical view hai, prediction ya guarantee nahi.

Data kahan se laayein

Ye app khud koi market data fetch, store ya share nahi karta - pura analysis aapke browser mein hota hai. Data ke free sources: NSE India ki official website ke historical data downloads, aapke broker app (Groww, Zerodha etc.) ke exports, ya kisi bhi source se manually note kiye hue prices. Jo file aap denge, usi ki latest date app dikhayega.
