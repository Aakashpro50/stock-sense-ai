# Stock Sense AI - NSE Stock Scanner

Free, fully client-side NSE stock analyzer.

Live site: https://aakashpro50.github.io/stock-sense-ai/

## What it does
- Upload or paste a historical CSV (symbol,date,open,high,low,close,volume) and every stock in the file gets scanned, or paste one stock's closing prices.
- Real technicals per stock: RSI(14), MACD(12,26,9), SMA 20/50/200, EMA20, 52-week range position, volume vs 20-day average.
- Transparent rule-based BUY / HOLD / SELL signals (9 checks, exact reasons shown per stock) plus short-term (2-6 week) and long-term (6-12 month) rule-based outlook.
- 6-month style chart with 20/50-day averages.

## Privacy
100% client-side. No market data is fetched, stored, uploaded or shared by this app - analysis runs entirely in the browser. No API keys, no backend, no tracking.

## Disclaimer
Educational/informational tool only. Rule-based technical signals are not investment advice. Markets carry risk - do your own research or consult a SEBI-registered advisor.
